## 1. Executive budget decision

The venture will use a **stage-gated, ₹50,000 cash budget** to validate a tightly scoped, offline-first prototype. Existing student-owned equipment, open-source software and confirmed university resources will be used wherever practical. Founder and student-team labour is treated as an in-kind contribution, not as free cash or revenue.

The budget is intended to produce evidence that the prototype can be installed offline, perform deterministic spreadsheet reconciliation, preserve row and monetary invariants, present uncertain matches for human review, export repeatable results and generate a tamper-evident audit record. It is **not** a budget to build a complete government platform, pay six founder salaries, handle live citizen records, or deliver a production procurement-ready system.

## 3. Scope funded by the ₹50,000 budget

The funded prototype should deliver:

1. An offline desktop workflow for importing two cycle-level spreadsheet files and mapping their columns.
2. Deterministic exact matching and candidate suggestions, with ambiguous cases routed to a human review queue.
3. Integer-paise financial calculations and a clear reconciliation/variance summary.
4. Multi-sheet spreadsheet export, including reviewed outcomes and unresolved exceptions.
5. An audit-event/hash-manifest demonstration with tests for payload modification and other defined tampering cases.
6. A reproducible synthetic dataset, automated test results, a clean-install demonstration, a known-limitations register and Hindi/English operating guidance.

**Explicit exclusions:** production deployment; payment authorisation; live Aadhaar or other sensitive identity data; real beneficiary-level data without written authority and approved safeguards; OCR; scheme-note drafting; paid cloud AI/API services; model training; full legal certification; and salaries at market rates. Any later scope expansion requires a separate budget and approvals.

## 4. Spending controls and stage gates

These are recommended internal controls; they do not presume that a grant provider will release funds in these exact tranches.

| Gate | Maximum cumulative spend | Evidence required before proceeding |
|---|---:|---|
| Gate 1 — Define and baseline | ₹12,500 | Approved scope, workflow map, synthetic test-data specification, item-level procurement list and named budget owner. |
| Gate 2 — Build and verify | ₹37,500 | Offline installer, pinned dependency inventory, repeatable test results, financial invariant checks and documented limitations. |
| Gate 3 — Demonstrate and close | ₹45,000 | Clean-install demonstration, reviewer feedback, user guidance, issue log and final validation report. |
| Controlled contingency | ₹50,000 maximum | Remaining ₹5,000 may be used only for justified, eligible expenses; record approval and receipts. |

Operational rules:

- No spending before grant sanction or written confirmation of the permitted expenditure rules.
- Keep receipts, quotations, an asset/media register and a simple ledger mapped to these nine budget heads.
- Seek multiple quotations where practical, especially for meaningful equipment or external services.
- Do not use the contingency to cover unrelated expenses or unapproved scope expansion.
- Keep demo datasets synthetic. Any future use of real or de-identified administrative records requires separate written permission, a defined purpose, access controls, retention/deletion rules and approval from the data owner.
- Report actual spending against budget after the prototype milestone; never present estimates as actual expenditure.

### Acceptance criteria for the funded milestone

- The application installs and runs on a clean test machine with no internet connection.
- Required spreadsheet rows are accounted for; invalid, unmatched, ambiguous and unsupported-cardinality cases remain visible rather than silently disappearing.
- Monetary parsing and totals are tested with valid and invalid examples using integer paise.
- Exported outputs agree with independently computed control totals on the golden test dataset.
- Audit testing records exactly which tampering operations were tested and detected; do not claim universal or “100%” tamper detection from a single example.
- The report states known limitations, including the handling of split payments, unresolved matches and any untested scale/performance conditions.

## 10. Explicit financial exclusions and limitations

The three-year figures exclude or do not yet fully quantify:

- Market-rate salaries for all six founders/team members and the opportunity cost of unpaid student time.
- Any full-time hire, office rent, major hardware purchase or extensive travel outside the planned regions.
- Tax payments, professional fees beyond the stated allowances, and final GST registration/treatment decisions.
- Delayed receivables beyond the included liquidity buffers, bad debts, financing costs and long public-sector procurement cycles.
- Formal third-party certification, penetration testing beyond the defined review, production incident response or insurance requirements.
- Significant customer-specific integrations, migration work, new modules, OCR, cloud services or use of live citizen records.

Therefore, the plan should use phrases such as **“illustrative cash scenario,” “budget ceiling,” “grant requested,” “pricing hypothesis,”** and **“subject to customer validation.”** Avoid stating “profitable by Year 3” as a verified outcome.
